What we export

Six produce lines, two origins, one point of contact

Our range is deliberately narrow. We stay close to the growers who supply these six crops so we can speak to quality, counts and availability without guessing.

Blueberries

Firm, high-brix fruit from highveld farms. Punnet-packed for retail or bulk for processing.

Jul – Nov 125g & 150g punnets Air freight

Strawberries

Tunnel and open-field fruit, picked to colour spec and cooled within hours of harvest.

Aug – Dec 250g & 500g punnets Air freight

Litchis

Mauritius and McLean’s Red varieties, offered sulphured or untreated to your market’s rules.

Nov – Jan 2kg & 5kg cartons Air & sea

Kiwis

Green-flesh Hayward from cooler production areas, graded by count and pressure-tested.

Apr – Jun 3.5kg single-layer trays Sea freight

Passion Fruit

Purple granadilla with deep colour and high pulp yield. Long window, strong juice demand.

Feb – Aug 2kg & 4kg cartons Air & sea

Avocados

Hass, Fuerte and Pinkerton, dry-matter tested before picking and shipped in reefer containers.

Mar – Oct 4kg cartons, counts 14–30 Sea freight

How we work

From the orchard to your clearing agent

Most of what can go wrong with fresh produce goes wrong between the pack house and the port. We keep a single team across that whole stretch, so there is one person accountable for the fruit, the temperature and the documents.

Enquiries get a written offer with variety, grade, pack, counts, available volume per week, Incoterms and lead time — so you can price it properly before committing.

Our export process

  • 1. Grower selection & allocation Volume booked with farms we have audited, ahead of the season.
  • 2. Grading & packing to spec Your counts, punnet sizes, labels and pallet configuration.
  • 3. Pre-cooling & cold storage Fruit pulled to temperature before it ever moves.
  • 4. Inspection & documentation Phytosanitary certificates, origin documents and export clearance.
  • 5. Freight & arrival support Air or reefer sea freight, with tracking and quality feedback on landing.

Markets

Where our fruit goes

We supply importers, wholesale markets and retail programmes, and we are set up to open new destinations where the phytosanitary protocol allows it.

Middle East

UAE and the wider Gulf — our most active berry and avocado destination, mainly by air.

Europe & UK

Counter-seasonal berry and avocado programmes into established import partners.

Asia

Growing demand for litchis, avocados and passion fruit where market access permits.

Regional SADC

Road-freight loads into neighbouring markets with short transit and quick turnaround.
